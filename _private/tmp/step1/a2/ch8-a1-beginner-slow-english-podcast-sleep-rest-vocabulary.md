Today I woke up late, not early, but late. I woke up around 10 a.m. I was so **sleepy**. I went to bed last night around 11 p.m., and I woke up at 10 a.m. That means I had 11 hours of sleep last night. I usually do not sleep that much, but last night I was really tired. I usually 《have》 a lot of **energy**, and I do not need too much sleep. But **lately**, maybe for the past three days, I have been feeling very tired and sleepy. I have not had so much energy lately. So I __take care of__ myself, and I try to 《get》 as much rest as I can. Like one of my students said, a good night of sleep is only half the battle. As long as I get a good night of sleep, I think everything else will be perfect. Luckily, I could __sleep in__ late this morning because I did not have any early morning lessons to teach. I could sleep in late because I did not have work. Otherwise, I normally get up at 8 a.m. to teach every morning. Sometimes it is so frustrating to __fall asleep__. Sometimes it is difficult for me to fall asleep at night. I just lie in bed, and I wait and wait. But sometimes I cannot fall asleep. It is especially frustrating when I have an early class in the morning. When I have to __wake up__ early, I do not get my full eight hours of sleep. Without my eight hours of sleep, I cannot function. I cannot go to the gym, teach my lessons, or __hang out__ with my friends. If I do not get my full eight hours of sleep, I feel very tired. How many hours of sleep do you need? Do you get enough sleep? I try to sleep early, around 10 p.m. Ten p.m. is early for me. On the weekends, like Saturday and Sunday, I do not work. I go to sleep around 2 or even 3 a.m. sometimes. But 4 a.m. is the latest time that I will go to sleep. If I go to sleep late, I usually try to 《take》 a short nap during the day. I like to take naps if I did not get a good night of sleep. But sometimes short naps 《make》 me even more sleepy. Do you like taking naps during the day? Or do naps make you feel more sleepy? I can take a 20-minute nap, and I will feel better. But if I take a 40-minute nap, I end up feeling more tired than I did before. I am so jealous of people who can fall asleep easily. Some people can fall asleep so quickly, in a matter of minutes. They can pass out and be in deep sleep. It is crazy. I wish I could fall asleep that fast. It is a true talent. The screen on my phone or computer wakes me up and keeps me awake. When I am sick, maybe I have a cough or I am feeling congested. I take medication to help with my sickness. The medication also helps me fall asleep. Some medicine can cause sleepiness or drowsiness. Drowsiness is the feeling of sleepiness. I love medicine that makes me feel drowsy. Then I am able to sleep so well. But once I took medicine that caused sleepiness, and that night I sleepwalked. I used to sleepwalk all the time when I was little. I would walk around and talk while I was still sleeping. One time, I woke up in my parents' bedroom. I was standing right in front of them, and I was just talking. My parents were so confused. When I woke up standing there, I was also confused. I did not know where I was or how I got there. Do you ever sleepwalk? Are you a sleepwalker? Tell me about a time when you sleepwalked at night. But I do not sleepwalk anymore. I only talk in my sleep sometimes. Sometimes it can scare people. People can get scared when I am talking alone at night. But I am just dreaming out loud. Do you ever talk in your sleep? Before sleeping, I always wash my face, brush my teeth, and set my alarm on my phone. Then I __go to bed__. What do you always do before going to bed? Listen every day and practice repeating all the new words. If you liked this video, like and subscribe for more content. You will probably like these videos as well. Thank you for watching. Bye.

| 單字 | 熟悉的意思 | 文中的用法 | 中文翻譯 |

| have | 有 | 表示吃喝、經歷或享有 | 喝；經歷；享有 |
| take | 拿 | 表示脫下、進行或照顧 | 脫下；進行；照顧 |
| make | 製作 | 表示產生、犯下或做出 | 產生；犯下；做出 |

# 6. 【D 超綱文法】

| 原句 | 文法名稱 | 結構拆解 | 為什麼這樣寫 | 中文翻譯 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hello, and welcome back to my slow English podcast. | 不定詞片語 | to + 原形動詞 | 表示目的、計畫或想做的事。 | 你好，歡迎回到我的慢速英語播客。 |
| Today I will be talking about sleep and rest. | 未來進行式 | will be + V-ing | 表示未來某段時間會正在進行的事。 | 今天我要談談睡眠和休息。 |
| That means I had 11 hours of sleep last night. | 關係子句 | 名詞 + who/which/that + 子句 | 補充說明前面的名詞。 | 這意味着我昨晚睡了11個小时。 |
| But lately, maybe for the past three days, I have been feeling very tired and sleepy. | 現在完成進行式 | have/has been + V-ing | 表示從過去持續到現在的動作或狀態。 | 但是最近，也许是過去三天，我一直感覺很累，很困。 |
