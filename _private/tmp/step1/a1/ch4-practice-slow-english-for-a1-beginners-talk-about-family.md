# 1. 【校稿後文本（含 A/B/C 標註）】

Hello, __welcome back__ to my slow English **podcast**. This **podcast** is for English **listening** **practice** for **beginners**. Today, I will talk about my family. My family is very important to me. I am very __close to__ my family. I have two **siblings**. I have one sister and one brother. My brother is 15 years old. He loves __playing video games__. He is much taller than me, but he does not 《look》 **grown-up**. He still __looks like__ a kid, a very tall kid. My sister is 23 years old. She studies **quantum** **physics** at **university**. She is also an English teacher. She is also taller than me. She likes to **travel**. She also likes to __play video games__. Do you have any **siblings**? What do they like to do? My mom is 42 years old. She is an English teacher. She teaches students in the United States. My dad is 51 years old. Guess what he 《does》 __for work__. He is also an English teacher. He teaches students in Mexico. What do your parents 《do》 __for work__? Do your parents __work in__ **construction**, **education**, **management**, or **healthcare**?

# 4. 【B 超綱片語】

| 片語 | 字面拆解 | 實際意思 | 中文翻譯 |
|------|----------|----------|----------|
| welcome back | welcome = 歡迎；back = 回來 | 歡迎某人回到節目、地方或活動 | 歡迎回來 |
| close to | close = 近；to = 到、對 | 和某人關係親近、感情好 | 和……感情親近 |
| play video games | play = 玩；video games = 電子遊戲 | 玩電玩或電子遊戲 | 玩電玩 |
| look like | look = 看；like = 像 | 外表或感覺像某人、某物 | 看起來像 |
| for work | for = 為了；work = 工作 | 為了工作、和工作有關 | 工作上；作為工作 |
| work in | work = 工作；in = 在……裡 | 在某個產業或領域工作 | 從事……方面的工作 |

# 5. 【C 特殊用法】

| 單字 | 熟悉的意思 | 文中的用法 | 中文翻譯 |
|------|------------|------------|----------|
| close | 關閉；近的 | close to my family 表示感情上的親近，不是空間距離近 | 和家人感情親近 |
| look | 看 | look + 形容詞表示「看起來……」，是在描述外表或給人的感覺 | 看起來不像大人 |
| do | 做 | do for work 中的 do 用來問一個人的職業或工作內容 | 做什麼工作 |

# 6. 【D 超綱文法】

| 原句 | 文法名稱 | 結構拆解 | 為什麼這樣寫 | 中文翻譯 |
|------|----------|----------|--------------|----------|
| He loves playing video games. | 動名詞作動詞受詞 | love + V-ing | playing video games 放在 loves 後面，表示他喜歡做「玩電玩」這件事。 | 他喜歡玩電玩。 |
| She likes to travel. | 不定詞作動詞受詞 | like + to + 原形動詞 | to travel 放在 likes 後面，表示她喜歡去做「旅行」這件事；同一句型也出現在 likes to play video games。 | 她喜歡旅行。 |
| He is much taller than me, but he does not look grown-up. | 比較級句型 | be + much + 形容詞比較級 + than + 比較對象 | taller than me 表示「比我高」，much 用來加強比較的程度，表示「高很多」。 | 他比我高很多，但是他看起來不像大人。 |
| He does not look grown-up. | 連綴動詞 look + 形容詞 | look + 形容詞 | look 後面接形容詞 grown-up，用來說明主詞看起來呈現某種狀態。 | 他看起來不像大人。 |
| Guess what he does for work. | 嵌入式 wh 子句 | guess + what + 主詞 + 動詞 | what he does for work 放在 guess 後面當內容，語序是 what + he + does，不是疑問句語序。 | 猜猜他的工作是什麼。 |
