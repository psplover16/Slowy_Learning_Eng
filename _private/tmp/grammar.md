# MissHoney Grammar Accumulation

## Grammar 1
- Structure: who 引出補充說明，說明是想學習的初學者。
- Explanation: 這個 podcast 是給想透過聽英文來學習的初學者。
- Sources:
- Source: a1/ch1-slow-english-for-beginners-a1-listening-practice | [Music] Hello, my name is Tyana Ortiz. | 這個 podcast 是給想透過聽英文來學習的初學者。
## Grammar 2
- Structure: by 後面接動名詞，表示透過某種方式。
- Explanation: 透過聽英文來學習。
- Sources:
- Source: a1/ch1-slow-english-for-beginners-a1-listening-practice | Welcome to my slow podcast. | 透過聽英文來學習。
## Grammar 3
- Structure: that 當受詞時可以省略，後面說明 something 是什麼。
- Explanation: routine 是你每天會做的事情。
- Sources:
- Source: a1/ch1-slow-english-for-beginners-a1-listening-practice | This podcast is for beginners who want to learn by listening to English. | routine 是你每天會做的事情。
## Grammar 4
- Structure: after 當介系詞時，後面可接動名詞表示在做完某事後。
- Explanation: 教學後，我讀書。
- Sources:
- Source: a1/ch1-slow-english-for-beginners-a1-listening-practice | Today's podcast is about my daily routine. | 教學後，我讀書。
## Grammar 5
- Structure: 一般現在式問句用 do 幫忙形成問題。
- Explanation: 你早餐通常吃什麼？
- Sources:
- Source: a1/ch1-slow-english-for-beginners-a1-listening-practice | My daily routine. | 你早餐通常吃什麼？
## Grammar 6
- Structure: stretching 是被喜歡的活動，所以用動名詞放在 love 後面。
- Explanation: 我喜歡每天伸展。
- Sources:
- Source: a1/ch1-slow-english-for-beginners-a1-listening-practice | A routine is something you do every day. | 我喜歡每天伸展。
## Grammar 7
- Structure: to dance 表示去跳舞這個活動，可放在 like 後面。
- Explanation: 你喜歡跳舞嗎？
- Sources:
- Source: a1/ch1-slow-english-for-beginners-a1-listening-practice | In the morning, I wake up at 7 a.m. I brush my teeth. | 你喜歡跳舞嗎？
## Grammar 8
- Structure: 口語英文常把 with 留在句尾，詢問和誰一起做某事。
- Explanation: 你和誰一起工作？
- Sources:
- Source: a1/ch1-slow-english-for-beginners-a1-listening-practice | I wash my face, and I eat breakfast. | 你和誰一起工作？
## What 引導的名詞子句作主詞
- Structure: What + 主詞 + 動詞 + 副詞/介系詞片語 + be + 補語
- Explanation: what 子句把「我最喜歡的事物」包成主詞，後面用 be 連接答案。
- Sources:
- Source: a1/ch2-beginner-english-slow-listening-practice-talking-about-me | What I love most about Mexico are the people and the food. | 我最喜歡墨西哥的是那裡的人和食物。
## 過去分詞片語作補充說明
- Structure: 主詞 + 動詞 + 過去分詞片語
- Explanation: surrounded by 說明慶祝時的狀態，相當於 while I am surrounded by my family and friends。
- Sources:
- Source: a1/ch2-beginner-english-slow-listening-practice-talking-about-me | I usually celebrate surrounded by my family and friends. | 我通常會在家人和朋友的陪伴中慶祝。
## 動名詞片語作受詞
- Structure: like + V-ing + 受詞/補語
- Explanation: 動詞 like 後面可以接 V-ing 當受詞，表示喜歡做某件事。
- Sources:
- Source: a1/ch2-beginner-english-slow-listening-practice-talking-about-me | I like surrounding myself with people who love love me. | 我喜歡讓自己身邊都是愛、愛我的人。

## 動名詞片語作介系詞受詞
- Structure: of + V-ing + 受詞
- Explanation: 介系詞 of 後面不能直接接動詞原形，所以用 understanding 形成名詞性片語。
- Sources:
- Source: a1/ch3-slow-english-listening-for-beginners-talking-about-languages | The feeling of understanding one sentence or one word feels so good. | 理解一個句子或一個單字的感覺非常好。
## 動名詞作動詞受詞
- Structure: love + V-ing
- Explanation: love 後面接 V-ing，表示喜歡做某件事。
- Sources:
- Source: a1/ch3-slow-english-listening-for-beginners-talking-about-languages | I personally love learning new languages. | 我個人喜歡學習新的語言。
- Source: a1/ch4-practice-slow-english-for-a1-beginners-talk-about-family | He loves playing video games. | 他喜歡玩電玩。

## teach + 受詞 + to V
- Structure: teach + someone + to + 原形動詞
- Explanation: 這個句型表示「教某人去做某事」，後面的 to understand 和 use 說明學生要學會做的事。
- Sources:
- Source: a1/ch3-slow-english-listening-for-beginners-talking-about-languages | I love teaching students to understand new words, to understand phrases, idioms, and eventually use them. | 我喜歡教學生理解新的單字、理解片語和慣用語，並且最後能使用它們。
## 假設語氣條件句
- Structure: If + 主詞 + could + 原形動詞, would + 主詞 + 原形動詞
- Explanation: 用 could 和 would 表示想像中的情況，不是在說真實現在或未來一定會發生的事。
- Sources:
- Source: a1/ch3-slow-english-listening-for-beginners-talking-about-languages | If you could download any language into your brain, would you do it? | 如果你可以把任何一種語言下載到你的腦中，你會這麼做嗎？
## would 表示假設中的選擇
- Structure: Wh 問句 + would + 主詞 + 原形動詞
- Explanation: would 讓問題停留在「如果可以選」的假設情境中，詢問對方偏好。
- Sources:
- Source: a1/ch3-slow-english-listening-for-beginners-talking-about-languages | Which would you prefer? | 你會比較喜歡哪一種？
## 不定詞作動詞受詞
- Structure: like + to + 原形動詞
- Explanation: to travel 放在 likes 後面，表示她喜歡去做「旅行」這件事；同一句型也出現在 likes to play video games。
- Sources:
- Source: a1/ch4-practice-slow-english-for-a1-beginners-talk-about-family | She likes to travel. | 她喜歡旅行。
## 比較級句型
- Structure: be + much + 形容詞比較級 + than + 比較對象
- Explanation: taller than me 表示「比我高」，much 用來加強比較的程度，表示「高很多」。
- Sources:
- Source: a1/ch4-practice-slow-english-for-a1-beginners-talk-about-family | He is much taller than me, but he does not look grown-up. | 他比我高很多，但是他看起來不像大人。
## 連綴動詞 look + 形容詞
- Structure: look + 形容詞
- Explanation: look 後面接形容詞 grown-up，用來說明主詞看起來呈現某種狀態。
- Sources:
- Source: a1/ch4-practice-slow-english-for-a1-beginners-talk-about-family | He does not look grown-up. | 他看起來不像大人。
## 嵌入式 wh 子句
- Structure: guess + what + 主詞 + 動詞

- Explanation: 把受詞放到句首強調「任何新的資格、經驗或職位」，後面再說明我會把它們加進履歷。
- Sources:
- Source: b1/ch5-job-interview-essentials-slow-english-podcast-for-intermediate-b1 | Any new qualifications, experience, or employment positions, I add to my CV resume right away. | 任何新的資格、經驗或工作職位，我都會立刻加到我的履歷裡。
## 現在完成式搭配 ever/never/only ever
- Structure: subject + have/has + adverb + past participle
- Explanation: 用現在完成式談到目前為止的人生經驗；only ever 強調「一直以來只」。
- Sources:
- Source: b1/ch5-job-interview-essentials-slow-english-podcast-for-intermediate-b1 | I have only ever been an English teacher. | 我一直都只是英文老師。
## It is + 名詞 + to V
- Structure: It + be + noun phrase + to + verb
- Explanation: It 是形式主詞，真正的主詞是後面的 to be able to communicate very well with others。
- Sources:
- Source: b1/ch5-job-interview-essentials-slow-english-podcast-for-intermediate-b1 | It is one of my greatest strengths to be able to communicate very well with others. | 能夠和別人非常良好地溝通，是我最大的優點之一。
## 同位語子句 that
- Structure: idea + that + subject + verb
- Explanation: that 子句說明 idea 的具體內容，也就是「我可以在同一個時刻做所有事」這個想法。
- Sources:
- Source: b1/ch5-job-interview-essentials-slow-english-podcast-for-intermediate-b1 | Some weaknesses of mine, however, are the idea that I can do everything in one moment. | 我的一些弱點是我會覺得自己可以在同一個時刻做所有事。
## 目的子句 so that
- Structure: main clause + so that + subject + verb
- Explanation: so that 引導目的，說明我限制自己的原因是為了不要最後感到過勞和疲累。
- Sources:
- Source: b1/ch5-job-interview-essentials-slow-english-podcast-for-intermediate-b1 | Now I try to limit myself so that I don't end up feeling overworked and tired. | 現在我試著限制自己，這樣我才不會最後覺得工作過度又疲累。
## 被動不定詞
- Structure: to be + past participle
- Explanation: to be hired 和 to be fired 表示「被錄取」和「被解雇」，重點在承受動作的人。
- Sources:
- Source: b1/ch5-job-interview-essentials-slow-english-podcast-for-intermediate-b1 | But just as it feels nice to be hired, it feels really bad to be fired. | 就像被錄取感覺很好一樣，被解雇的感覺真的很糟。

- Explanation: 表示從過去到現在曾經有過被某人這樣對待的經驗。
- Sources:
- Source: b1/ch9-speak-like-a-native-common-english-idioms-slow-english-podcast-for-a2-b1-beginners | I've also been thrown under the bus myself. | 我自己也被推去擋刀過。
## Noun clause after be
- Structure: subject + be + that + clause
- Explanation: that 後面的完整子句說明 punishment 的內容是什麼。
- Sources:
- Source: b1/ch9-speak-like-a-native-common-english-idioms-slow-english-podcast-for-a2-b1-beginners | Our punishment was that we couldn't watch TV in the basement for two weeks. | 我們的處罰是兩個星期不能在地下室看電視。
## Infinitive phrase as subject
- Structure: To + base verb phrase + be + complement
- Explanation: To blow off steam 整個不定詞片語當主詞，用來定義這個片語的意思。
- Sources:
- Source: b1/ch9-speak-like-a-native-common-english-idioms-slow-english-podcast-for-a2-b1-beginners | To blow off steam is to do something that releases stress or tension. | 發洩壓力是指做某件事來釋放壓力或緊繃。
## Find oneself + -ing
- Structure: find + reflexive pronoun + adverb + -ing
- Explanation: 表示發現自己常常處在某個行為或狀態中。
- Sources:
- Source: b1/ch9-speak-like-a-native-common-english-idioms-slow-english-podcast-for-a2-b1-beginners | I find myself constantly blowing off steam. | 我發現自己經常在發洩壓力。
## Purpose phrase with in order to
- Structure: in order to + not + base verb; by + -ing
- Explanation: in order to 表示目的；by blowing off steam 表示用什麼方式達成目的。
- Sources:
- Source: b1/ch9-speak-like-a-native-common-english-idioms-slow-english-podcast-for-a2-b1-beginners | In order to not take it out on my friends or family, I prevent it by blowing off steam every day. | 為了不要把情緒發洩在朋友或家人身上，我每天透過發洩壓力來預防這件事。
## Keep on + gerund
- Structure: keep/kept on + V-ing
- Explanation: 表示某個動作持續發生，常帶有一直發生、令人困擾的感覺。
- Sources:
- Source: b1/ch9-speak-like-a-native-common-english-idioms-slow-english-podcast-for-a2-b1-beginners | My camera kept on freezing. | 我的鏡頭一直凍結。
## Embedded thought with relative clause
- Structure: thing + that + I thought + clause + was + noun
- Explanation: that 引導的子句修飾 thing；I thought 後面再嵌入 I wouldn't be able to... 這個想法內容。
- Sources:
- Source: b1/ch9-speak-like-a-native-common-english-idioms-slow-english-podcast-for-a2-b1-beginners | Another thing that I thought I wouldn't be able to pull off was a shaved head. | 另一件我原本以為自己無法駕馭的事是光頭。
## Zero conditional for definitions
- Structure: If + simple present, simple present
- Explanation: 用簡單現在式說明一般情況或定義。
- Sources:
- Source: b1/ch9-speak-like-a-native-common-english-idioms-slow-english-podcast-for-a2-b1-beginners | If you're on the same page with someone, it means you both understand each other. | 如果你和某人在同一頁上，意思是你們彼此理解。
## Wh-question with reflexive pronoun and future time phrase
- Structure: Where + do + subject + verb + reflexive pronoun + future time phrase?
- Explanation: see yourself 表示想像自己處在某種狀態，in five years 表示五年後。
- Sources:
- Source: b1/ch10-learn-english-with-slow-interviews-comprehensible-input-b1-interview-practice | Where do you see yourself in five years? | 你覺得五年後的自己會在哪裡？
## By + gerund for method
- Structure: by + V-ing
- Explanation: by listening 表示練習英文聽力的方法是聽慢速英語對話。

## Even though concession clause
- Structure: even though + subject + verb
- Explanation: even though 用來表示讓步，意思是「即使」。
- Sources:
- Source: b1/ch10-learn-english-with-slow-interviews-comprehensible-input-b1-interview-practice | I personally get really nervous at job interviews, even though I'm a hard worker and a good learner, and I'm always on time. | 即使我是個努力工作的人、學習能力也好，而且總是準時。
## If-clause as noun clause
- Structure: determine + if + subject + verb
- Explanation: 這裡的 if 不是條件，而是 whether 的意思，作為 determine 的受詞。
- Sources:
- Source: b1/ch10-learn-english-with-slow-interviews-comprehensible-input-b1-interview-practice | They need to determine if you have extensive experience or minimal experience. | 他們需要判斷你是有豐富經驗還是經驗很少。
## Present perfect with for
- Structure: have/has + past participle + for + time period
- Explanation: 用現在完成式表示從過去開始並持續到現在的狀態。

- Structure: has/have been + 過去分詞

## what 名詞子句作主詞
- Structure: What + 主詞 + 動詞 + the most + be + 補語
- Explanation: 把最喜歡的事情整體放在主詞位置，再用 was 說出答案。
- Sources:
- Source: b1/ch11-slow-english-podcast-for-high-beginners-a2-b1-my-trip-to-the-usa-comprehensible-input | What I enjoyed the most about living in the United States, specifically Nebraska, was the seasons. | 我在美國，尤其是內布拉斯加，生活時最喜歡的是季節。
## even though 讓步副詞子句
- Structure: even though + 子句, 主句

## Perfect Gerund
- Structure: preposition + having + past participle
- Explanation: having had 表示在小時候已經擁有過某人、事、地或故事，現在回頭感到感恩。

- Explanation: 用過去進行式描述當時正在發生的背景動作，再接另一個發生的動作。
- Sources:
- Source: b1/ch18-english-kitchen-vocabulary-comprehensible-input | As I was walking home, I realized I forgot to buy a dustpan. | 我走回家的時候，才發現我忘了買畚斗。
## 間接問句語序
- Structure: 疑問詞 + 主詞 + 助動詞 + 原形動詞
- Explanation: what else you can peel 是嵌入在句子裡的問題，所以用 you can，不用 can you。
- Sources:
- Source: b1/ch18-english-kitchen-vocabulary-comprehensible-input | Let me know in the comments what else you can peel! | 請在留言區告訴我，你還可以削什麼！
## This is my first time + V-ing
- Structure: This is + 所有格 + first time + V-ing
- Explanation: 用來表示某人第一次做某件事或有某種經驗。
- Sources:
