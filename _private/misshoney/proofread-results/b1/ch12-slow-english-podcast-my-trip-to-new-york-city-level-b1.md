# 【D 超綱文法】

| It was the first time that I had ever been to Cane's. | the first time + Past Perfect | It was the first time that + subject + had ever + past participle | 因為敘述的是過去的第一次經驗，所以用 had ever been 表示在那之前從來沒有去過。 | 那是我第一次去 Cane's。 |

| I've been dreaming of this moment for so long. | Present Perfect Continuous | Subject + have/has been + V-ing + for + duration | have been dreaming 表示從過去到現在一直期待這件事，for so long 表示持續很久。 | 我一直夢想著這一刻很久了。 |
