# 【校稿後文本（含 A/B/C 標註）】

Hello, welcome back to my slow English podcast, a podcast for English **intermediate** listeners. Today, I want to practice some common job interview **vocabulary**, some different phrases you might need at a job interview, and a story about my experience with job interviews. I always keep my **resume** __up to date__. Any new **qualifications**, experience, or **employment** **positions**, I add to my **CV** **resume** __right away__. I never __hand in__ an old **resume**. How often do you update your **resume**? How important is it for you to keep your **resume** __up to date__? I have __worked in__ a few different 《areas》: education, **translation**, and **transcription**. But mostly, I have only ever been an English teacher. I have only ever had **part-time** jobs. I have never had a **full-time** job. Do you have a few **part-time** jobs or one **full-time** job? I prefer **part-time** jobs because my **schedule** needs to be **flexible**. I travel a lot, so I need to **manage** and change my **schedule** a lot. The different __time zones__ in the world can be difficult to **manage** with a **full-time** job. I think one of my biggest skills is my social skills. It is one of my greatest **strengths** to be able to communicate very well with others. What are some of your greatest **strengths**? What are some of your skills? Some **weaknesses** of mine, however, are the idea that I can do everything in one moment. I can't **manage** my time, and I sometimes don't sleep or rest, and I __end up__ feeling **overworked** and tired. Now I try to limit myself so that I don't __end up__ feeling **overworked** and tired. What do you consider to be your **weaknesses**? Have you ever __ended up__ feeling **overworked** and **exhausted**? It feels nice to be **hired**. For someone to say, "You're **hired**," is nice. But just as it feels nice to be **hired**, it feels really bad to be **fired**. I got **fired** one year ago, and it was awful. I felt so 《lost》, and I felt like a **failure**. But it's important to remember that whether you're **hired** or **fired**, you are not a **failure**. **Success** is a feeling you can 《give》 yourself. You **define** your own **success**: not a company, not a **paycheck**, not money, not anybody but yourself. Have you ever been **fired** before? How did it feel, and what did you do to feel better? Tell me about your dream job. And if you liked this video, like and comment down below so I can make more practice videos like this in the future.

# 【完整中文翻譯】

哈囉，歡迎回到我的慢速英語 Podcast，這是一個給英語中級聽眾的 Podcast。今天，我想練習一些常見的求職面試詞彙、一些你在求職面試中可能會需要的不同片語，還有一個關於我求職面試經驗的故事。我總是讓我的履歷保持最新。任何新的資格、經驗或工作職位，我都會立刻加到我的 CV 履歷裡。我從不繳交舊履歷。你多久更新一次履歷？對你來說，讓履歷保持最新有多重要？我曾經在幾個不同領域工作過：教育、翻譯和逐字稿轉寫。但大多時候，我一直都只是英文老師。我一直都只有兼職工作。我從來沒有全職工作過。你有幾份兼職工作，還是一份全職工作？我比較喜歡兼職工作，因為我的行程需要有彈性。我常常旅行，所以我需要常常管理和更改我的行程。世界上不同的時區，對全職工作來說可能很難處理。我覺得我最大的技能之一是我的社交能力。能夠和別人非常良好地溝通，是我最大的優點之一。你有哪些最大的優點？你有哪些技能？然而，我的一些弱點是我會覺得自己可以在同一個時刻做所有事。我不會管理自己的時間，而且我有時不睡覺或休息，最後就會覺得工作過度又疲累。現在我試著限制自己，這樣我才不會最後覺得工作過度又疲累。你認為自己的弱點是什麼？你曾經最後覺得工作過度又精疲力盡嗎？被錄取的感覺很好。有人對你說：「你被錄取了，」感覺很好。但是，就像被錄取感覺很好一樣，被解雇的感覺真的很糟。我一年前被解雇了，那很可怕。我覺得很迷失，也覺得自己像個失敗者。但重要的是要記得，不管你是被錄取還是被解雇，你都不是失敗者。成功是一種你可以給自己的感覺。你定義自己的成功：不是公司，不是薪水支票，不是金錢，不是任何人，而是你自己。你以前曾經被解雇過嗎？那感覺如何？你做了什麼讓自己好過一點？告訴我你的夢想工作。如果你喜歡這支影片，請按讚並在下方留言，這樣我未來就能製作更多像這樣的練習影片。

# 【D 超綱文法】

| 原句 | 文法名稱 | 結構拆解 | 為什麼這樣寫 | 中文翻譯 |
|------|----------|----------|--------------|----------|
| Today, I want to practice some common job interview vocabulary, some different phrases you might need at a job interview, and a story about my experience with job interviews. | 關係代名詞省略 | phrases + (that/which) + you might need | you might need 前面省略了 that 或 which，用來補充說明 phrases 是「你可能會需要的」片語。 | 一些你在求職面試中可能會需要的不同片語 |
| Any new qualifications, experience, or employment positions, I add to my CV resume right away. | 前置受詞 | Object + subject + verb | 把受詞放到句首強調「任何新的資格、經驗或職位」，後面再說明我會把它們加進履歷。 | 任何新的資格、經驗或工作職位，我都會立刻加到我的履歷裡。 |
| I have only ever been an English teacher. | 現在完成式搭配 ever/never/only ever | subject + have/has + adverb + past participle | 用現在完成式談到目前為止的人生經驗；only ever 強調「一直以來只」。 | 我一直都只是英文老師。 |
| It is one of my greatest strengths to be able to communicate very well with others. | It is + 名詞 + to V | It + be + noun phrase + to + verb | It 是形式主詞，真正的主詞是後面的 to be able to communicate very well with others。 | 能夠和別人非常良好地溝通，是我最大的優點之一。 |
| Some weaknesses of mine, however, are the idea that I can do everything in one moment. | 同位語子句 that | idea + that + subject + verb | that 子句說明 idea 的具體內容，也就是「我可以在同一個時刻做所有事」這個想法。 | 我的一些弱點是我會覺得自己可以在同一個時刻做所有事。 |
| Now I try to limit myself so that I don't end up feeling overworked and tired. | 目的子句 so that | main clause + so that + subject + verb | so that 引導目的，說明我限制自己的原因是為了不要最後感到過勞和疲累。 | 現在我試著限制自己，這樣我才不會最後覺得工作過度又疲累。 |
| Have you ever ended up feeling overworked and exhausted? | 現在完成式疑問句 | Have/Has + subject + past participle...? | 用來詢問到目前為止的人生經驗，ever 強調「曾經」。 | 你曾經最後覺得工作過度又精疲力盡嗎？ |
| But just as it feels nice to be hired, it feels really bad to be fired. | 被動不定詞 | to be + past participle | to be hired 和 to be fired 表示「被錄取」和「被解雇」，重點在承受動作的人。 | 就像被錄取感覺很好一樣，被解雇的感覺真的很糟。 |
| Whether you're hired or fired, you are not a failure. | whether A or B | whether + clause + or + clause | whether A or B 表示不管是 A 還是 B，後面的結果都一樣。 | 不管你是被錄取還是被解雇，你都不是失敗者。 |
| Success is a feeling you can give yourself. | 關係代名詞省略 | a feeling + (that/which) + subject + verb | you can give yourself 前面省略 that 或 which，用來說明 feeling 是「你可以給自己的」感覺。 | 成功是一種你可以給自己的感覺。 |
| And if you liked this video, like and comment down below so I can make more practice videos like this in the future. | 條件子句 if | if + subject + verb, main clause | if 引導條件，表示在「你喜歡這支影片」的情況下，請按讚並留言。 | 如果你喜歡這支影片，請按讚並在下方留言。 |
