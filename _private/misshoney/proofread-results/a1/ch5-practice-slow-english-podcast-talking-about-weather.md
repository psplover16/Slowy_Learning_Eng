## 1. 【校稿後文本（含 A/B/C 標註）】

Hello, and __welcome back__ to my slow English **podcast**, a **podcast** to practice English listening for **beginners**. Today, I will __talk about__ the weather. I like hot weather. I __prefer hot weather over cold weather__. What do you **prefer**, the **heat** or the 《cold》? I love sunny days. Sunny days are **bright** and warm. On sunny days, I like to read outside. Do you like sunny days? What do you do on sunny days? I like rainy days. On rainy days, the sky is gray. When it rains, I **usually** __stay inside__. I don't like to __go outside__ when it's rainy. What do you prefer: to stay inside or go outside when it's rainy? I stay inside. I like to watch movies or __TV series__, movies 《like》 Love, Rosie, Inside Out, and Spirited Away, and **series** 《like》 Gossip Girl and You. On rainy days, I also love to read books or draw. I read books or draw inside when it's rainy, not outside. Do you like rainy days? What do you usually do on rainy days? I don't like **windy** days. On windy days, the wind is so strong. The wind **blows** **everywhere**. I prefer to stay inside on windy days. Do you like windy days? What can you do on windy days? I love **snowy** days. On snowy days, the **ground** is 《all》 white. It **shines** **brightly** and **beautifully**. I like to __act like a kid__ in the snow. I like to __make snow angels__. Do you like snowy days? What can you do on snowy days? My **favorite** days are sunny days. I love when it's hot. I love the heat. What are your favorite days? What is your favorite weather? In my city, we don't have snowy days. In my city, it doesn't snow. I __used to live in__ Nebraska, United States. In Nebraska, it is snowy. It is very snowy in the winter. Do you have snowy days in your city? | 片語 | 字面拆解 | 實際意思 | 中文翻譯 |

| prefer A over B | prefer + A + over + B | 比起 B 更喜歡 A | 比起冷天更喜歡熱天 |
| stay inside | stay + inside | 待在室內 | 待在室內 |
| go outside | go + outside | 出去；到外面去 | 出去 |

## 5. 【C 特殊用法】

| 單字 | 熟悉的意思 | 文中的用法 | 中文翻譯 |
|------|------------|------------|----------|
| cold | 冷的 | the cold 中作名詞，指寒冷或冷天氣 | 寒冷 |
| like | 喜歡 | 用來表示「像、例如」，後面接例子 | 像 Love, Rosie 這類電影 |
| all | 全部 | 放在形容詞前，表示「完全、整個都」 | 全白 |
| get | 得到 | get + 形容詞，表示「變得」 | 變得很乾 |

## 6. 【D 超綱文法】

| 原句 | 文法名稱 | 結構拆解 | 為什麼這樣寫 | 中文翻譯 |
|------|----------|----------|--------------|----------|
| Hello, and welcome back to my slow English podcast, a podcast to practice English listening for beginners. | to 不定詞表示用途 | 名詞 + to + 原形動詞 | 用 to practice 說明這個 podcast 的用途 | 你好，歡迎回到我的慢速英語 podcast，這是一個給初學者練習英文聽力的 podcast。 |
| When it rains, I usually stay inside. | when 引導時間副詞子句 | When + 主詞 + 動詞, 主詞 + 動詞 | 先說「什麼時候」，再說那時通常會做什麼 | 下雨時，我通常待在室內。 |
| I love when it's hot. | when 子句作受詞 | love + when + 主詞 + 動詞 | 用整個 when 子句當 love 的內容，表示喜歡某種情況 | 我喜歡天氣熱的時候。 |
| In my city, it doesn't snow. | 天氣用虛主詞 it | it + 天氣動詞 | 英文談天氣常用 it 當主詞，it 不指特定東西 | 在我的城市，不會下雪。 |
| I used to live in Nebraska, United States. | used to + 原形動詞 | 主詞 + used to + 原形動詞 | 表示過去曾經如此，現在不一定如此 | 我以前住在美國內布拉斯加州。 |
| What's the weather like in your city? | What is ... like? 問狀況 | What + be 動詞 + 名詞 + like? | 用 like 問「是什麼樣子」或「狀況如何」 | 你城市的天氣如何？ |
| Try to pause the video, and try not to read the subtitles. | try to V / try not to V | try + to + 原形動詞；try + not + to + 原形動詞 | 表示「試著做某事」或「試著不要做某事」 | 試著暫停影片，並試著不要看字幕。 |
| Don't forget to like and subscribe for more content. | 否定祈使句 + forget to V | Don't + 原形動詞；forget + to + 原形動詞 | 用來提醒對方不要忘記做某件事 | 別忘了按讚並訂閱更多內容。 |
