# 【校稿後文本（含 A/B/C 標註）】

Hello and __welcome back__ to my Slow English **podcast**. Today we will be reading one of my **favorite** books. It's 《called》 The Giving Tree. Read slowly with me. The Giving Tree.

Once there was a tree, and she loved a little boy.
And every day the boy 《would》 come, and he 《would》 **gather** all her leaves and make them into crowns and play the king of the forest.
He 《would》 climb up her **trunk** and swing from her **branches** and eat apples.
And they 《would》 play __hide-and-go-seek__.
And when he was tired, he 《would》 sleep in her **shade**.
And the boy loved the tree very much.
And the tree was happy.

But __time went by__, and the boy grew older, and the tree was often alone.
Then one day the boy came to the tree, and the tree said, "Come, boy, come and climb up my trunk and swing from my branches and eat apples and play in my shade and be happy."
"I am too big to climb and play," said the boy.
"I want to buy things and have fun. I want some money. Can you give me some money?"
"I'm sorry," said the tree, "but I have no money. I have only leaves and apples. Take my apples, boy, and sell them in the city. Then you will have money, and you will be happy."
And so the boy climbed up the tree and **gathered** her apples and carried them away.
And the tree was happy.

But the boy __stayed away__ for a long time, and the tree was sad.
And then one day the boy __came back__.
The tree shook with joy, and she said, "Come, boy. Climb up my trunk and swing from my branches and be happy."
"I am too busy to climb trees," said the boy.
"I want a house to keep me warm," he said.
"I want a wife, and I want children, and so I need a house. Can you give me a house?"
"I have no house," said the tree.
"The forest is my house, but you may __cut off__ my branches and build a house. Then you will be happy."
And so the boy __cut off__ her branches and carried them away to build his house.
And the tree was happy, but the boy stayed away for a long time.

And when he came back, the tree was so happy.
She could **hardly** speak.
"Come, boy," she **whispered**.
"Come and play."
"I am too old and sad to play," said the boy.
"I want a boat that will take me __far away__ from here. Can you give me a boat?"
"__Cut down__ my trunk and make a boat," said the tree.
"Then you can sail away and be happy."
And so the boy __cut down__ her trunk, made a boat, and sailed away.
And the tree was happy, but not really.

And after a long time, the boy came back again.
"I am sorry, boy," said the tree.
"But I have nothing 《left》 to give you. My apples are gone."
"My teeth are too weak for apples," said the boy.
"My branches are gone," said the tree.
"You cannot swing on them."
"I am too old to swing on branches," said the boy.
"My trunk is gone," said the tree.
"You cannot climb."
"I am too tired to climb," said the boy.
"I am sorry," sighed the tree.
"I wish that I could give you something, but I have nothing left. I'm just an old **stump**. I am sorry."
"I don't need very much now," said the boy.
"Just a quiet place to sit and rest. I am very tired."
"Well," said the tree, straightening herself up as much as she could.
"Well, an old stump is good for sitting and resting. Come, boy, sit down and rest."
And the boy 《did》.
And the tree was happy.

The end.

Why or why not?
Was the boy **selfish** or just human?
Have you ever had someone in your life who gave to you like the tree gave to the boy?
What do you think the **moral** of this story is?
I'll read your **comments**.
Have a wonderful day.
I'll see you next time with another story to tell.

# 【完整中文翻譯】

哈囉，歡迎回到我的慢速英文 podcast。今天我們要讀我最喜歡的書之一。它叫做《愛心樹》。跟我一起慢慢讀。《愛心樹》。

從前有一棵樹，她愛著一個小男孩。每天男孩都會來，他會收集她所有的葉子，把它們做成王冠，然後扮演森林之王。他會爬上她的樹幹，從她的樹枝上盪來盪去，還會吃蘋果。他們會玩捉迷藏。當他累了，他會睡在她的樹蔭下。男孩非常愛這棵樹。樹也很快樂。

但是時間過去了，男孩長大了，樹常常獨自一人。後來有一天，男孩來到樹旁，樹說：「來吧，孩子，來爬上我的樹幹，從我的樹枝上盪來盪去，吃蘋果，在我的樹蔭下玩耍，開開心心的。」「我太大了，不能爬樹和玩了，」男孩說。「我想買東西，也想玩得開心。我想要一些錢。你可以給我一些錢嗎？」「對不起，」樹說，「可是我沒有錢。我只有葉子和蘋果。拿我的蘋果去吧，孩子，把它們拿到城裡賣掉。那樣你就會有錢，也會快樂。」於是男孩爬上樹，收集她的蘋果，並把它們帶走。樹很快樂。

但是男孩離開了很久，樹很難過。後來有一天，男孩回來了。樹高興得發抖，她說：「來吧，孩子。爬上我的樹幹，從我的樹枝上盪來盪去，開開心心的。」「我太忙了，不能爬樹，」男孩說。「我想要一間房子讓我保暖，」他說。「我想要一位妻子，也想要孩子，所以我需要一間房子。你可以給我一間房子嗎？」「我沒有房子，」樹說。「森林就是我的房子，但你可以砍下我的樹枝，蓋一間房子。那樣你就會快樂。」於是男孩砍下她的樹枝，並把它們帶走去蓋他的房子。樹很快樂，但是男孩又離開了很久。

當他回來時，樹非常快樂，幾乎說不出話來。「來吧，孩子，」她低聲說。「來玩吧。」「我太老也太難過了，不能玩了，」男孩說。「我想要一艘船，能帶我離開這裡，到很遠的地方。你可以給我一艘船嗎？」「砍下我的樹幹做成船吧，」樹說。「那樣你就可以航行離開，也會快樂。」於是男孩砍下她的樹幹，做成一艘船，然後航行離開。樹很快樂，但其實不是真的快樂。

過了很久以後，男孩又回來了。「對不起，孩子，」樹說。「可是我沒有東西可以給你了。我的蘋果沒有了。」「我的牙齒太弱，不能吃蘋果了，」男孩說。「我的樹枝沒有了，」樹說。「你不能在它們上面盪了。」「我太老了，不能在樹枝上盪了，」男孩說。「我的樹幹沒有了，」樹說。「你不能爬了。」「我太累了，不能爬了，」男孩說。「對不起，」樹嘆氣說。「我希望我能給你一些東西，但我什麼都沒有了。我只是一個老樹樁。對不起。」「我現在不需要太多，」男孩說。「只要一個安靜的地方坐下來休息。我很累了。」「嗯，」樹說，盡可能把自己挺直。「嗯，老樹樁很適合坐著休息。來吧，孩子，坐下來休息。」男孩照做了。樹很快樂。

結束。你覺得《愛心樹》怎麼樣？你認為樹到最後是快樂的嗎？為什麼，或為什麼不？男孩是自私，還是只是人性如此？你生命中是否曾經有某個人像樹給予男孩那樣給予你？你認為這個故事的寓意是什麼？我會讀你們的留言。祝你有美好的一天。下次我會帶另一個故事再見。

# 【D 超綱文法】

| 原句 | 文法名稱 | 結構拆解 | 為什麼這樣寫 | 中文翻譯 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Today we will be reading one of my favorite books. | 未來進行式 | will be + V-ing | 表示接下來一段時間會進行的活動 | 今天我們要讀我最喜歡的書之一。 |
| Once there was a tree, and she loved a little boy. | There be 句型 | There was + 名詞 | 用來引出故事中存在的人事物 | 從前有一棵樹，她愛著一個小男孩。 |
| And every day the boy would come. | would 表過去習慣 | would + 原形動詞 | 說過去常常重複發生的事情 | 每天男孩都會來。 |
| I am too big to climb and play. | too ... to ... | too + 形容詞 + to + 原形動詞 | 表示太怎樣而不能做某事 | 我太大了，不能爬和玩。 |
| I want a boat that will take me far away from here. | 關係子句 | 名詞 + that + 子句 | that 後面補充說明 boat 是怎樣的船 | 我想要一艘能帶我離開這裡到很遠地方的船。 |
| I wish that I could give you something. | wish + could | wish that + 主詞 + could + 原形動詞 | 表示現在做不到但希望能做到的事 | 我希望我能給你一些東西。 |
| Have you ever had someone in your life who gave to you like the tree gave to the boy? | 現在完成式與關係子句 | Have + 主詞 + ever + p.p.; someone + who + 子句 | ever 詢問到目前為止的經驗，who 補充說明 someone | 你生命中是否曾經有某個人像樹給予男孩那樣給予你？ |
| What do you think the moral of this story is? | 插入問句的間接問句語序 | What do you think + 主詞 + 動詞 | do you think 插入後，後面的 the moral ... is 保持陳述句語序 | 你認為這個故事的寓意是什麼？ |
