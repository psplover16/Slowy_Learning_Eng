# 1. 【校稿後文本（含 A/B/C 標註）】

Hello, __welcome back__ to my __slow English podcast__. This **podcast** is a **comprehensible** and clear way of listening to English. Practice repeating any words you do not understand. First, try to listen without reading the **subtitles**. Today I want to talk to you about common **conversations** and words you can use at the **airport**. When I arrive at the airport, I always 《make》 sure I 《have》 my **passport**, my __boarding pass__, and all of my **luggage**. Those three things are **essential** to check. I triple-check before I leave my house and when I arrive at the airport. Once I 《get》 to the **security** **checkpoint**, I show my boarding pass to the security guard. They point me to which line I should go to, so I stand in line and wait. I __take off__ my shoes, and I 《take》 off my coat. I take all my **electronics** out of my bag. If I have headphones, a **laptop**, my phone, or even my **chargers**, I put them all in a **basket**. Then I walk through the __metal detector__. I do not usually get checked for extra security, but sometimes they check with a little metal detector. They check to see if I have any metal on my body. They will say, "Can you please step to the side?" Then they scan me with their little metal detector. Once I had a big bottle of sunscreen lotion. They threw the whole thing in the garbage. It was my fault because it was over 100 milliliters. But I was still so angry. They could have kept it for themselves or given it to somebody else. It was very frustrating. Have you ever had anything taken away from you at the security checkpoint? Have you ever been stopped and scanned at the security checkpoint? After I get through the checkpoint, I have to find my gate number. Usually my gate number is on my boarding pass. If it is not there, I check the display screens at the airport. I always check to see if the time and the gate number have changed. I always make sure to ask the service agents if I have any questions at all. Once I find my gate, I like to wait until the very end to start boarding. I do not like standing and waiting in line. My seat number is usually one of the last ones to board the flight, so I sit down anyway. I show my boarding pass to one of the flight attendants, and I find my seat. I love the window seat. I always prefer the window seat over the middle seat. For long flights, I prefer the **aisle** seat. The aisle seat is perfect for longer flights because I can go to the bathroom whenever I want. For short flights, I do prefer the window seat. On arrival to my destination, the pilot announces that we will be landing soon. I start to get all my things ready. If I have a connecting flight, then I am ready to hurry to my next flight. Connections can be very stressful if you do not have much time to switch flights. When I do not have a connecting flight, I usually just sit, relax, and wait. I eventually get through customs and immigration. If I have a checked bag or checked baggage, I go to the luggage carousel. At the luggage carousel, I look for my luggage. After I find my luggage, I order an Uber, a taxi, or a bus. Then I head out to my accommodation. Airports can be very stressful, but they are also very exciting. New adventures, new places, and new people can make travel exciting. Knowing the names of these different things at the airport can make your trip less stressful and more **relaxed**. Hopefully these new words help your English listening skills. I hope they help you have a stress-free trip at the airport. Like this video so I know you want more content like this. Subscribe and turn on notifications to see every video I post. Practice English with me every Monday and Wednesday. See you next time. Bye.

# 6. 【D 超綱文法】

| 原句 | 文法名稱 | 結構拆解 | 為什麼這樣寫 | 中文翻譯 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hello, welcome back to my slow English podcast. | 不定詞片語 | to + 原形動詞 | 表示目的、計畫或想做的事。 | 你好，歡迎回到我的慢速英語播客。 |
| They point me to which line I should go to, so I stand in line and wait. | 關係子句 | 名詞 + who/which/that + 子句 | 補充說明前面的名詞。 | 他們告诉我應該去哪条線，所以我就排队等待。 |
| I always check to see if the time and the gate number have changed. | 現在完成式 | have/has + 過去分詞 | 連結過去經驗和現在。 | 我總是檢查时間和登機口號碼是否改變。 |
| On arrival to my destination, the pilot announces that we will be landing soon. | 未來進行式 | will be + V-ing | 表示未來某段時間會正在進行的事。 | 到達目的地後，飛行員宣布我們即将着陆。 |
