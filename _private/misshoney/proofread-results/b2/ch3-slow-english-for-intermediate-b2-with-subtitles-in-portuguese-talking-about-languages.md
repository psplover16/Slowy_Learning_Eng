# 1. 【校稿後文本（含 A/B/C 標註）】

Hello, __welcome back__ to my __Slow English podcast__, a **podcast** for English listening for beginners. This **podcast** is level B1. Today I will __talk about__ **languages**. I speak two **languages**: English and Spanish. When speaking, I feel almost like I have two separate **personalities**, one in English and one in Spanish. The **distinction** between each **language** is not just the way I speak, but also the volume of my voice. I am much louder in English than I am in Spanish. However, in Spanish, I have a much more outgoing **personality**. It's weird how a **language** can change your **personality**. English is my __first language__. I speak it with my family at home and whenever I teach English. But when I speak Spanish, I have only ever spoken at school or with my friends. Today, I only ever speak English with my friends. I believe that's why I have a lot more casual **vocabulary** in Spanish than I do in English. My Spanish is a lot more __laid-back__ than my English. At least, that's why I think I have two different **personalities** __in each language__. What about you? Do you ever feel like you have two different **personalities** __in each language__?

# 2. 【完整中文翻譯】

大家好，歡迎回到我的慢速英語podcast，這是一​​個針對初學者英語聽力的podcast。 該podcast為B1級。 今天我要談談語言。 我會說兩種語言：英語和西班牙語。 說話時，我覺得自己幾乎有兩種不同的性格，一種是英語，一種是西班牙語。 每種語言之間的差異不僅在於我說話的方式，還在於我聲音的音量。 我說英語比說西班牙語大聲很多。 然而，在西班牙文中，我的個性比較外向。 語言能改變你的性格，這很奇怪。 英語是我的第一語言。 我在家和教英文時都和家人說這句話。 但是當我說西班牙語時，我只在學校或與朋友說過。 今天，我只和朋友說英語。 我相信這就是為什麼我在西班牙語中比在英語中擁有更多的休閒詞彙。 我的西班牙語比英語輕鬆許多。 至少，這就是為什麼我認為每種語言我都有兩種不同的性格。 你呢？ 你有沒有覺得每種語言都有兩種不同的個性？
